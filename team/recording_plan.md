# Own-video recording plan (Step 13)

36 clips in total, 12 per member. 720p or higher, phone held still (tripod or propped up).
Fall onto a mattress or thick mat. File name: M<member>_<day|night>_<type>_<nn>.mp4

| Member | Daylight falls (4) | Night clips (4) | Hard negatives, no fall (3) | Two people (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | from standing, walking, chair, bed | 2 falls + 2 normal (lights off, lamp only) | sit down fast, lie down on purpose, bend | one of two falls |
| M2 | from standing, walking, chair, bed | 2 falls + 2 normal (phone screen light only) | push-ups, pick something up, sit on floor | one of two falls |
| M3 | from standing, walking, chair, bed | 2 falls + 2 normal (street or corridor at night) | lie on a bed, crouch, tie shoes | one of two falls |

Also: at least 4 clips shot from far away (person small in frame) and 4 from a high angle.
After recording, fill in splits/own_videos_labels.csv (one row per clip):
  light = day / night, type = fall / adl, fall_from = standing / walking / chair / bed / none,
  fall_start_s = second the fall starts (blank for adl), people = 1 or 2,
  distance = near / far, angle = eye / high.
Own videos are TEST ONLY: never used for training or tuning.
